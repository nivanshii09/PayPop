# 💜 PayPop

### Recover more. Stress less.

PayPop is an AI-powered revenue recovery platform designed to help businesses recover revenue from failed and abandoned payments.

Instead of treating every failed payment the same way, PayPop analyzes payment context, determines the risk, recommends the most appropriate recovery action, validates that action through safety rules, and records every decision for complete traceability.

---

## ✨ Features

- 📊 Real-time revenue recovery dashboard
- 💳 Failed and abandoned payment monitoring
- 🤖 AI-powered recovery recommendations
- 🛡️ Safety engine to prevent unsafe automated actions
- 🔄 Smart recovery actions such as retry, payment link, reminder, and escalation
- 👤 Customer profiles and payment history
- 📈 Recovery analytics and performance metrics
- 📝 Complete audit trail of AI decisions and recovery actions
- ⚡ Responsive modern fintech interface
- 🧪 Mock/demo recovery mode for safe testing
